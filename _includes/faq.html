What are your starting prices?+

Interior Detail starts at $100, Exterior Detail at $50, Full Detail at $140, and Wax and Polish at $50. These are starting prices, not final quotes. Vehicle size, condition, and the work needed determine your final quote.

Why are some services quote-only?+

Paint Protection and Headlight Restoration are quoted individually because the condition of the vehicle and the amount of preparation or restoration can vary significantly. Tell us about your vehicle so we can discuss the work needed.

Do you come to my location?+

Yes. We provide mobile detailing at your home, workplace, or another approved location in Rocky Hill, CT and surrounding towns. Send your ZIP code to check availability. Any travel charge will be included in your quote before you confirm.

Does the form confirm my appointment?+

No. The form prepares an email request; you still need to open your email app and press Send. An appointment is confirmed only after you approve the quote and we confirm the date and time.

How can I leave a review?+

Use the review form to choose a rating and share your experience, then send the prepared email. All ratings are welcome. Public display is optional, and reviews are not automatically posted.
